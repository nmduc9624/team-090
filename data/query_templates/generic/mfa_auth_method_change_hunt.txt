# Generic MFA Authentication Method Change Hunt
telemetry: auth, cloud_audit
fields: timestamp, actor, user, target, src_ip, user_agent, application, action, status, result
logic:
  from auth or cloud_audit within last 24h
  where action indicates MFA disabled, authentication method removed, security info changed, strong auth disabled, or recovery method changed
  project timestamp, actor, user, target, src_ip, user_agent, application, action, status, result
correlate:
  review successful logins and sensitive app access before and after the authentication method change.
